motir.co/design — the public design showcase

The page where a visitor moves three controls and the site they are standing on becomes a different-looking product. It is a marketing / credibility surface, not a token reference: the argument is “the design system Motir gives you is the one Motir wears.” Everything here composes into the shipped motir.co chrome and renders from @motir/design-system@0.1.0, which this repository already installs.

Every string is placeholder at the specified register — the words are MOTIR-3862's. Every number in this asset was measured in a browser, not read off a file; the method is in design-notes.md.
