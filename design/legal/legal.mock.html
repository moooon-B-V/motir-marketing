An unknown slug — 404, inside the chrome

SUPERSEDED by MOTIR-4245 · MOTIR-4247 SUPERSEDED — a RECORD, not a spec. This body was never built and is not to be built. An unknown legal slug is served by the SITE-WIDE not-found room (`app/not-found.tsx`, shipped by MOTIR-4193, drawn by MOTIR-4245 in motir-core/design/public-site/) — Next resolves `notFound()` to the nearest `not-found.tsx` above the route, and this repository has exactly one.
