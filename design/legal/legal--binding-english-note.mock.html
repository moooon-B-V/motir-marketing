motir.co/legal — the binding-English note

A delta of design/legal/legal.mock.html. In the ten non-English locales, a legal document and the legal index open under a short note in the page’s language: the documents are published in English only, the English text is the binding version, and (on a document) a link to that document’s English address. English shows no note. Only the panels that change are drawn; the chrome and the reading column are composed from the base asset and from the shipped header and footer.
